Tiny drivers. Huge drifts.

Free · desktop & phone · keyboard, gamepad or touch · source on GitHub

Features

Drift boosts

Hold drift through a corner to charge blue, orange, then purple sparks. Let go for a turbo. Nail the countdown for a Rocket Start.

Grand Prix

Race all 4 tracks for points (10-8-6-4-2-1). Finish in the top 3 overall to win a trophy at the ceremony.

Time trial + ghost

Set a record and your ghost is saved. Next time you race against your own see-through best lap.

5 AI rivals

The AI follows racing lines, drifts, uses items and rubber-bands gently, so races stay close.

Touch controls

Auto-gas with big on-screen steer, drift, brake and item buttons (multi-touch). Phones work in portrait or landscape.

All made in code

Karts, tracks, scenery, UI art and sound effects are generated at runtime. There are no image or audio files.

Controls

KeyboardW/↑ gas · S/↓ brake · AD/←→ steer · Space/Shift drift · E/J item · Esc/P pause · M mute
GamepadRT gas · LT brake · stick steer · RB drift · X/LB item · Start pause
TouchGas is automatic. Steer < > on the left, DRIFT / BRAKE / ITEM on the right. Tap II to pause.
